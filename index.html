02 — HOW WE SHARE

A private publishing workflow

From our stage
to your feed.

EncoreStage uses a private internal content-production and publishing workflow to prepare and publish EncoreStage media across supported social platforms.

For TikTok, authorization connects EncoreStage's own creator account so the team can manage and publish its own content. It is an internal workflow—not a consumer-facing app—and it does not provide TikTok account access to the public.
